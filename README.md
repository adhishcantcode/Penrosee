## About the Game

**PENROSE** is a first-person psychological horror game where reality bends, architecture defies logic, and every step takes you deeper into an endless nightmare.

Trapped inside an impossible staircase inspired by the **Penrose Stairs**, you must navigate an environment caught in an infinite loop, uncover disturbing anomalies, and find a way to escape.

But there's a catch.

**The staircase remembers your mistakes.**

Every loop is a test of perception. Every anomaly is a piece of the puzzle. And every wrong decision brings you closer to the Void.

*Will you break the loop, or become part of it?*

## Core Gameplay

### The Infinite Staircase
Explore a seemingly endless staircase where the beginning and end are connected. Seamless spatial transitions create the illusion of an impossible architectural loop.

### Anomaly Detection
Something is wrong with this place.

Observe your surroundings carefully and identify environmental anomalies. Objects may change, disappear, or behave in ways that defy reality.

### Flashlight-Based Investigation
Your flashlight is more than a source of light.

Use it to reveal and interact with anomalies hidden throughout the staircase. But be careful — your battery won't last forever.

### Absorb the Anomalies
Aim your flashlight at an anomaly and hold **E** to absorb it. Successfully collect six anomalies to progress toward escaping the nightmare.

### Three Mistakes. One Fate.
You have only three chances to make mistakes.

Fail to identify the anomalies correctly, and the Void will claim you.

## Technical Highlights

- **Seamless Staircase Loop:** Teleportation-based spatial transitions designed to create the illusion of infinite architecture.
- **Dynamic Anomaly System:** Anomaly management with normal and altered object states.
- **Flashlight Interaction:** Raycast-based anomaly detection linked to flashlight activation.
- **Resource Management:** Battery consumption while the flashlight is active.
- **Progression System:** Track collected anomalies and player mistakes.
- **Psychological Horror Effects:** Screen glitches, environmental manipulation, and unsettling visual feedback.
- **Multiple Gameplay Outcomes:** Escape the loop or be consumed by the Void.

## Game Objective

Your mission is simple:

1. Navigate the endless staircase.
2. Observe your surroundings for anomalies.
3. Use your flashlight to locate them.
4. Absorb six anomalies.
5. Avoid making three mistakes.
6. Break the loop and escape.

**Remember: Not everything you see can be trusted.**

## Development

PENROSE was created around the theme **LOOP**, exploring how repeating environments, subtle visual changes, and limited resources can create psychological tension.

Rather than relying solely on jump scares, the game focuses on observation, uncertainty, and the fear of making the wrong decision.
